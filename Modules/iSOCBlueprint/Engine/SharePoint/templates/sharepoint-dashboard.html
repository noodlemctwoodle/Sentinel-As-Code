Estate flow

Source families → ingestion → detection → alerts → incidents over the last 30 days. Detection counts the tables an enabled Sentinel rule or Defender XDR custom detection reads. Alerts split in two: Defender XDR's, raised by its products (which read no workspace table), and Sentinel's, from the scheduled and NRT rules that fired; both feed the incidents. Strand width is GB; dashed strands are billed but read by no detection. Select a family for its tables and its product alerts.

Detection matrix

Every enabled analytics rule and Defender XDR custom detection that reads a workspace table: the data it reads, its health (Working: raised alerts or incidents in 30 days; Silent: raised none; Failing: had run failures in 30 days) and its incidents in 30 days by verdict. Columns are source families, and a number means the rule reads that many of the family's tables; Every table shows them one by one. Failing rules come first, then working, then silent, the most incidents first. Hover a column to see every rule that reads it; the footer counts them. Select a rule to open it in Rule Performance.

Ingest & billing flow

Ingested volume by source family → table → billing plan and lake tier → billed in full or eligible for a data grant, from the cost estimate over the last 30 days. Strand width is GB; each plan's ring is its share of the GB shown. Select a family to see every one of its tables.
